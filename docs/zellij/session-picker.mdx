---
title: "Session picker"
description: "Switch to another running workspace without stopping its programs."
---

Press **Ctrl-f Ctrl-w** to open the session picker. Type to filter, inspect the selected session's tab-tree preview, and press **Enter** to switch. **Esc** or **Ctrl-c** cancels.

## Example

Keep `work` and `personal` sessions running. Press **Ctrl-f Ctrl-w**, type `personal`, and press **Enter**. Return to `work` with the same picker. Both sessions retain their shells, tabs, and running programs.

The picker lists **running sessions only**. To find an exited session offered for recovery, open the session manager with **Ctrl-f I** instead.

Use [creating and renaming sessions](sessions.mdx) for a new workspace, or **Ctrl-f d** to detach the current client while leaving its session running.
