---
title: "Detach one client"
description: "Disconnect one attached view without closing its session."
---

Press **Ctrl-f D** to open the attached-client chooser. Select the view you want to disconnect and press **Enter**. **Esc** or **Ctrl-c** cancels.

Use the focused tab and pane title to identify the view. Only the selected attachment disconnects. Its session, running programs, and other viewers remain alive.

## Example

If the same session is open in two terminal windows, use **Ctrl-f D** to disconnect the spare window while continuing work in the other one.

**Ctrl-f d** is the quicker choice when you want to detach your own current view. Closing a pane with **Ctrl-f x** is different: it closes that pane and its program.

If the list cannot be loaded, check for an unanswered permission prompt in each attached view. Cancel and reopen the chooser after an attachment changes. It needs the modern configuration.
