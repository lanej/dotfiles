---
title: "Move a pane"
description: "Move the current pane into a new or existing tab."
---

Press **Ctrl-f m** to choose a destination for the focused pane. Select **New Tab** or an existing tab and press **Enter**. **Esc** or **Ctrl-c** cancels; selecting the current tab does nothing.

The pane moves with its running program, working directory, and scrollback. It is not replaced by a fresh shell.

## Example

If a test runner is taking space beside your editor, focus it, press **Ctrl-f m**, and choose **New Tab**. The test runner continues there while your editor remains in the original tab.

**Ctrl-f !** is the direct shortcut for moving the focused pane to a new tab without opening the menu. To gather every pane into another tab, use [gather panes](gather-panes.mdx).

Destinations are limited to the current session. Moving a live pane into another session is not supported. The destination picker needs the modern configuration.
