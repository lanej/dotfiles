# Automatic merging enabled

The effective switch permits Octo’s automatic merge workflow once its applicable gates and owner assessment are satisfied. This setting is not evidence that the PR is ready, merged, or authorized for unrelated publication. Inspect current action evidence and follow installed project guidance. Keep local checks, hosted checks, owner assessment, queued operations, and verified merge outcomes distinct.

An explicit per-PR setting overrides inheritance. A change to an inherited effective switch invalidates readiness; inspect current evidence before relying on a previous assessment. Do not duplicate a queued merge after an uncertain outcome.
