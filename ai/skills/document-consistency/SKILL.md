# Document consistency

Check the edited material and its dependent claims after the final relevant change. Preserve the author’s meaning and the requested scope. Use the project’s selected tools to render and inspect the delivered artifact when appearance or rendered content matters.

Read [document dependencies and citations](references/editing.md) before finalizing a document or configuration edit.
