# Research

Choose sources that can answer the actual question. Prefer the relevant code or primary
records for internal facts and authoritative sources for external claims. Use available
connectors and tools; do not assume a particular integration exists.

Handle bounded lookups directly. Delegate a self-contained investigation when isolation
or parallel work offers a concrete benefit, subject to the session's delegation rules.
The number of searches alone does not determine whether to delegate.

An optional research brief:

- Context: the decision or task the research supports.
- Question: the specific uncertainty to resolve.
- Sources: relevant sources and access constraints.
- Output: a concise synthesis, evidence pointers, gaps, and confidence.

Distinguish observed facts, inference, conflicting evidence, and unavailable information.
Return findings that answer the question, with enough source context to check consequential
claims. State what remains unresolved rather than treating an unsuccessful search as proof
of absence.
